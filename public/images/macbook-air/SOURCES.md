## Model and geometry reference

Official current specifications: https://www.apple.com/macbook-air/specs/

Apple sells M5 Air in 13-inch and 15-inch sizes (actual display diagonals 13.6 and 15.3 inches), not 14-inch. For the 13-inch version: body 30.41 cm wide × 21.5 cm deep × 1.13 cm high; 78 ANSI keys including 12 full-height function keys; Touch ID and Force Touch trackpad. “No keyboard-side speaker grilles” describes the visible case design, not absence of internal speakers: the 13-inch model has a four-speaker sound system.
