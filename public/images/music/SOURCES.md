## Player and collection behavior

Official embed documentation: https://artists.apple.com/support/1117-apple-music-marketing-tools

The component loads a single official Apple Music player only after the visitor clicks Listen here. Switching records removes the old iframe and stops its playback; selecting a record does not start audio. The native player controls playback. No account login, private library API, automatic sync, or simulated playback state is claimed. Direct Apple Music links remain available without JavaScript.

The collection contains 333 confirmed favorite records copied from the user-authorized native Music Favorites view, preserving its source order. All 327 previously exported favorite records were matched to that complete view, and the exact six missing records were added from the visible collection metadata. The site count comes from the imported data length. Duplicate source records are preserved. The search index includes only public song metadata. Unmatched records link to Apple Music search, explicitly labeled FIND, rather than invented catalog IDs.
